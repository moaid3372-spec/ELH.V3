FAQ

Questions Parents Ask

Which grades does ELH support?

ELH supports all nine grades: Primary 1 through Primary 6 and Prep 1 through Prep 3, with a British English curriculum.

How does the placement test work?

After registration, your child is automatically guided to a friendly AI placement test that identifies their real level and builds a personal learning path — no stress, no failing.

How will I follow my child's progress?

Through your Parent Dashboard and regular WhatsApp updates: progress reports, homework tracking, learning recommendations and child development insights.

Is ELH safe and child-friendly?

Yes. ELH is designed with child psychologists, follows strict privacy and security standards, and every AI interaction is designed to encourage — never to shame.

Does ELH work on mobile?

Absolutely. The experience is fully responsive on desktop, tablet and mobile, in both portrait and landscape, with dark and light modes.
